## Flutter vs React Native

Many developers and tech leaders face this "Flutter vs. React Native" dilemma when choosing a tech stack for mobile app development. The decision isn't always obvious, because both frameworks are capable, popular, and evolving quickly. Here’s a breakdown of why the struggle exists and how to think clearly about the decision.


##### Why They struggle?

1. Both Are Cross-Platform and Promise Faster Development
2. Uncertainty About Long-Term Support
3. Team Skills and Background
4. Rapid Evolution and Shifting Trends

<br/>

###### Let me point out few comparison,

Developer skills : React Native (if your team is already proficient in JavaScript/TypeScript you no need to hire mobile engineer)

Perfomance :  Flutter is a winner here (especially for high-performance or graphics-intensive apps)

UI and Design: Flutter (if you want pixel-perfect, consistent cross-platform UI)


Ecosystem and Libraries : I woud say both Flutter and React Native communities are growing and has very good support from communities.


Testing and CI/CD: Of course flutter. i have tried this and flutter has out of the box test support
